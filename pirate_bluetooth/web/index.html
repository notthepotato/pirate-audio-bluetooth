            Send new music from this PC by itself

            A small helper for Windows watches your music folders and sends new songs to the Pi in the background: songs you buy in iTunes or the Apple Music app, and anything you put in Music\Pirate Audio (handy for Bandcamp or SoundCloud downloads).

            1. Download the helper
            2. In the Downloads folder, click in the address bar, type powershell and press Enter. Then run:
              powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File pirate-sync.ps1 -Install
            3. That's it: it starts with Windows from now on. To stop it: the same line with -Uninstall.

            Download from the Pi Download all

              Songs are saved in the Music folder on the Pi. In Mopidy, find them under Browse → Local media (or Files).
